FlightMed Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

FlightMed does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. The app has no account, makes no network connections, and has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

FlightMed is a run log and clinical reference app for air medical crews, published by Ryan Duke. You can reach us at rad2040@live.com.

What the app stores, and where

While you use FlightMed it keeps the following on your own iPhone, inside the app's private storage:

  • The run in progress: run number, run type, the times you stamp, the medications, infusions and interventions you log, and the patient details you enter (gender, age, height and weight).
  • Reports you choose to save as PDF files.
  • Your settings, such as night mode and the values last used in the O₂ calculator.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. FlightMed has no server, no account system and no way to read what you have entered.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party services.
  • It does not track you, and it does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other part of your phone.

Sharing a report

A report leaves your phone only when you tap Share PDF and pick where to send it, such as Mail, Messages or AirDrop. That choice is yours, and the app you send it to handles the file under its own policies. We never receive it.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS may include FlightMed's data in that backup, depending on your settings. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a saved report from the Saved list in the app.
  • Choose New mission from the menu to clear the run in progress.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it has stored on the phone.

Patient information

FlightMed is a reference and logging tool. It is not the patient care report and is not a medical records system. Follow your agency's policies on what patient information may be recorded or kept on a personal device.

Children

FlightMed is written for medical professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy
