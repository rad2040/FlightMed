FlightMed Support

A run log and clinical reference for air medical crews.

Contact

Email rad2040@live.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Reporting a dose or content error

If a dose, rate or setting looks wrong, email us with the drug name and the screen it appeared on, so we can look into it. The doses, rates and settings in FlightMed are for general reference only. Double-check all information against your own Standards of Care before you use it. FlightMed does not replace your protocols, your clinical judgment or your medical control.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. FlightMed has no account, makes no network connections and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. The whole app runs on the phone, so it works in flight and anywhere else with no coverage.

How do I save a report and open it later?

On the Report tab, tap Save to phone. The report is saved as a PDF named with the date and run number. Tap Saved to open it again, even after the app has been closed or the phone restarted. To delete one, open the Saved list and delete it there.

How do I start over?

Tap the menu button at the top right and choose New mission. This clears the run in progress. Saved reports are kept.

Why does the app ask for no permissions?

It does not need any. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Night mode

Turn on NVG / night mode from the start screen or the menu. It shows amber on black so the screen does not wash out night-vision goggles.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
